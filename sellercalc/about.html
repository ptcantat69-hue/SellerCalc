ABOUT US

Clear numbers for independent businesses.

SellerCalc is a collection of straightforward, free calculators designed to help independent sellers, makers, freelancers and small business owners make more informed decisions.

Our approach

We believe useful business tools should be easy to understand and accessible without an account. Our calculators provide estimates based on the figures you enter, with explanations to help you interpret the results.

Important note

SellerCalc provides general educational tools. It does not provide accounting, tax, legal or financial advice. Always verify calculations against your own circumstances and local requirements.
